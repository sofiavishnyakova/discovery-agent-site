Interview plan · 3 of 20 questions

  1. An invoice was booked as NLT-09411 instead of NLT-0941 and the system accepted it. How would anyone find that today?

    Tests: wrong invoice numbers go unnoticed until a supplier complains.

  2. We assumed 350 invoices a month. How many does the team actually enter, and what happens at month-end?

    Tests: the volume behind every cost figure.

  3. Neither clerk opened the purchase-order system before submitting. Should a bill stop when it doesn't match its PO?

    Tests: where a human approval has to stay.
Privacy

Observed, not watched

People agree to a study when it's clear what is recorded, they stay in control, and nobody gets graded.

Recorded

  • Clicks and page changes
  • Switching between apps
  • Copy and paste between systems
  • Error messages the app shows
  • Screenshots, kept as evidence

Never recorded

  • What people type
  • Passwords, which are blacked out on screen
  • Anything while paused
  • Apps you haven't approved
  • Scores or rankings of individuals
